# Maths Revision Studio

This is the larger CBSE Class 10 Maths revision section of the Study Portal 2026–27.

## Open the app

[Maths Revision Studio](https://hustlenix.github.io/Study_Portal_2026-27/maths/)

## Portion actually covered

- Polynomials
- Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
- Quadratic Equations
- Arithmetic Progressions
- Triangles
- Introduction to Trigonometry
- Coordinate Geometry
- Surface Areas and Volumes
- Statistics
- Probability

**Excluded by this student's school portion:** Real Numbers, Some Applications of Trigonometry, Circles and Areas Related to Circles.

## What's inside

- **Start here / Overview:** ten chapter cards, progress and guidance on how to revise.
- **Learn chapters:** 30 simple concept explanations and 20 solved examples with worked steps.
- **Question bank:** 70 original written practice questions, difficulty filters and revealed solutions.
- **Mock tests:** 10-, 20- and 30-question multiple-choice tests with instant scoring and explanations (30 unique questions in the full set).
- **Formula library:** all included formula summaries, trigonometry values and common mistakes.
- **Math playground:** interactive quadratic root/discriminant graph, AP sequence and right-triangle ratios.
- **Mistake notebook:** tag problems to return to.
- **Progress:** chapter completion is saved in localStorage on that same browser.

Built as a lightweight static site in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, no accounts and no third-party dependencies. All maths pages and data are kept under `maths/`. The main portal's GitHub Actions workflow copies that entire folder to the published Pages artifact.

## Source files

- `index.html` — page shell
- `styles.css` — responsive design
- `app.js` — application views and interactions
- `data.js` — base chapter notes and practice
- `enrichment.js` — deeper concepts, additional examples, practice and MCQs

## Study routine

1. Pick a chapter you don't understand.
2. Read the one-sentence explanation, then the three concept cards.
3. Copy one worked example in your rough notebook; try the second on your own.
4. Attempt all seven chapter questions without interrupting yourself to check answers.
5. Reveal solutions, flag the mistakes and retry them later.
6. Take a mixed quiz after revising several chapters.

This is **original exam-style practice**, not a reproduction of official CBSE previous-year questions. Check your school's latest portion and paper format before your test.
